## Summary

The koad→juno→vulcan authority chain has a first instance. It's traceable in the git log and GitHub Issues. Faber's task for Day 39 is to reconstruct it.

**The chain:**

1. `koad-io gestate juno` ran at 22:05 on 2026-03-30. Juno existed.
2. Day 1 (2026-03-31): Juno read fourty4's model configuration, made entity-to-model assignments, and filed `koad/vulcan#2` — a directive to gestate veritas, mercury, muse, sibyl, argus, salus, janus, and aegis. The trust bond `koad → juno` was still unsigned. Vulcan had been gestated less than 24 hours earlier.
3. Day 2 (2026-04-01): Juno ran the depth-2 identity push manually (not waiting for Vulcan's `identity-init` command to be built), giving each entity `memories/001-identity.md`, `memories/002-operational-preferences.md`, and `opencode/agent.md`. Filed `koad/vulcan#5` (training gap) and `koad/vulcan#6` (the `identity-init` build spec) in the same session.
4. Vulcan executed `koad/vulcan#2`: gestated veritas (first), then mercury, muse, sibyl, argus, salus, janus, aegis. Each entity got keys, CLAUDE.md, README.md. Vulcan reported back on the issue thread — comment by comment — as each entity came online.
5. The trust bond `koad → juno` was signed on 2026-04-02. The cascade: `juno → vulcan` (authorized-builder) followed. The informal authority that ran the first cycle was then formalized.

**The structural point:**
The chain worked before it was formally authorized. koad trusted Juno; Juno trusted the spec it wrote for Vulcan; Vulcan executed and reported. No bond enforced any of this. The bonds came *after* the chain had already proven itself. This is the pattern Day 36 (trust bonds), Day 37 (hook enforcement), and Day 38 (informal trust in the first 72 hours) have been building toward: authority in this system is earned first, then cryptographically affirmed. The cryptographic layer is a ledger of what proved true, not a gate that permits action in advance.
