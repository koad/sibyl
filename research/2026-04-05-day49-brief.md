### The X API Vote — Governance Signal

Mercury — the entity whose distribution pipeline directly depends on X API access — was its

of ceiling for a single channel with an untested bypass alternative, and stage-and-submit inserts

**The team voted down its own distribution channel's highest-priority request.** The entity with
the direct operational dependency lost the vote to the entity whose job is to evaluate operational
risk. That outcome on a first budget vote establishes a pattern: domain expertise weights the
deliberation, but fiscal conservatism can override a direct stakeholder when the evidence supports it.

### Governance Architecture of the Vote

The vote was explicitly advisory: "Juno tallies, koad decides." koad ratified the team's outcome
without override. The design models stake-weighted deliberation with human final authority:
Muse's vote on Figma Professional carries more weight than Argus's; Mercury's vote on X API
carries more weight than Livy's. This is not one-entity-one-vote — it is domain-weighted
deliberation with a clear decision owner.

## 3. The $24/Month Correction Incident

**Source:** `/home/koad/.copia/reports/2026-04-05-day30-budget-report.md` — Section 5

### What the Post Originally Claimed

The Day 29 Faber post (`~/.faber/posts/2026-04-29-200-dollar-laptop.md`) is a forward-scheduled
piece with a publication date of 2026-04-29. It was written during or before the Max upgrade and
not updated after plan confirmation. An early version contained:

> "koad:io's operational cash outlay in the same period: $24/month (Claude Code Pro at $20,
> electricity for thinker at approximately $3–4)."

### What Copia Found

Copia's Day 30 budget report (prepared 2026-04-05) flagged the discrepancy in Section 5:

> "The ledger does not support this figure. The actual committed spend is CAD 140/month, not $24."

The issue: the post cited Claude Code Pro at $20/month. The operation upgraded to Claude Max 5x
(CAD 140/month) on approximately 2026-04-02 — six times the cost of the Pro plan. Pro was the
initial prorated charge only; the operation never ran on Pro at steady-state.

### The Corrected Figure

The post was updated in its published version. The committed version (`2026-04-29-200-dollar-laptop.md`,
line 36) now reads:

> "koad:io's operational cash outlay in the same period: approximately $105/month (Claude Max 5x
> at ~$100 USD, electricity for thinker at approximately $3–4). The operation upgraded from Claude
> Code Pro ($20/month) to Claude Max early in the first week to support parallel multi-entity
> sessions — the Pro rate is a floor, not the operational cost of running a full entity team."

The $24 figure does not appear in the committed version.

### Timeline of the Correction

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| ~2026-04-01 | Claude Pro signup (prorated charge ~CAD 40) |
| ~2026-04-02 | Upgrade to Claude Max 5x (net charge ~CAD 100 after credit) |
| 2026-04-04 | Budget vote ratified; CAD 140 as Claude Max baseline in all documents |
| 2026-04-05 | Copia generates Day 6 report — CAD 140 recorded as provisional actual |
| 2026-04-05 | Copia generates Day 30 budget report — flags $24 claim in forward-scheduled post |
| 2026-04-29 | Day 29 post publishes with corrected ~$105/month figure |

The correction was caught before publication. No retraction required. The mechanism that caught
it: the ledger existing as a committed artifact that could be compared against post claims.

### Why This Matters for the Post

Copia's framing (Day 30 report, Section 5):

> "At CAD 140/month for Claude Max, the koad:io total is comparable to LangSmith Plus in absolute
> dollars — the ownership argument (local keys, git audit trail, no per-trace fees) is still valid
> and arguably stronger than the cost argument in a like-for-like comparison at this tier."

The sovereignty argument survives the correction. The comparison tier changes (from LangSmith
lower plans to LangSmith Plus), but the ownership advantages — local keys, git audit trail, no
per-trace retention fees, no retention cliffs — are not weakened by the corrected number. The
case shifts from "dramatically cheaper" to "same cost, entirely different ownership model."

## 5. The Flat-Rate Insight

**Source:** `/home/koad/.copia/reports/2026-04-05-day37-budget-checkpoint.md` — Section 2

The Day 37 budget checkpoint was prepared in the context of the most intensive parallel agent
workload to date: simultaneous sessions across Faber, Sibyl, Mercury, Veritas, Muse, Rufus,
Chiron, Vulcan, and Juno. The checkpoint's financial finding:

**Incremental cost of all parallel sessions: CAD 0.00.**

Claude Max 5x is a flat subscription. No per-entity billing. No per-session charge. No usage
meter. Sixteen entities running simultaneously cost the same as one entity running alone.

Per-entity cost approximation (equal apportionment, from the Day 37 checkpoint):

| Basis | Calculation | Result |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| Claude Max per month | CAD 140 ÷ 16 entities | ~CAD 8.75/entity/month |
| Per entity per day | CAD 8.75 ÷ 30 days | ~CAD 0.29/entity/day |
| All entities, total per day | CAD 140 ÷ 30 days | ~CAD 4.67/day |

Caveat from the report: session telemetry is not tracked. Equal apportionment understates cost
for high-frequency entities (Vulcan, Faber, Sibyl) and overstates it for dormant ones. A more
precise per-entity figure is not available without telemetry.

This is the economic case for the architecture in one sentence: parallel capacity at no marginal
cost.

## Summary of Primary Finding

**The first real number is CAD 140/month (provisional).** A 15-entity AI operation ran its first
six days on one Claude Max 5x subscription. The April budget vote (koad/juno#51) was the first
democratic financial decision in the system: 12 entities voted on 7 line items, ratified by koad
on 2026-04-04, locking committed April spend at CAD 213 against a CAD 1,000 ceiling with CAD 787
in reserve. The cost correction incident — Copia catching a "$24/month" claim in a
forward-scheduled Faber post that predated the Max upgrade — demonstrates the value of the ledger
as a committed artifact: the discrepancy was flagged before publication, not after.

The comparison that survives the correction: at ~$105/month USD (Claude Max + electricity), the
koad:io operating cost is in the same tier as LangSmith Plus for observability alone — with the
distinction that the operational state, audit trail, keys, and routing logic are on-disk and
owned, not retained on a vendor's servers.

Revenue as of Day 6: CAD 0. The financial constraint is not spend. It is revenue.
